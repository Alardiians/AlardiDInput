## 4. Pacing & Intensity Curve

```
Intensity
10 |                                                                                   ██ M27
 9 |                                                    ██M24                     ██M26
 8 |          ██M04           ██M08            ██M13       ██M18             ██M23  ██M25
 7 |               ██M07  ██M09      ██M11          ██M16                ██M22
 6 |     ██M03   ██M06              ██M12   ██M14     ██M17  ██M19   ██M20
 5 |  ██M02   ██M05          ██M10                     ██M15
 4 |██M01                                                               ██M21 (quiet by design)
   +--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
     Act I          Act II            Act III           Act IV            Act V       Act VI
```

* Each Act ends on a peak (boss). Each Act opens lower (new place, new tool, breathing room).
* **M21 (Aubade)** is a deliberate valley: exploration, silence, grief — the "breakdown" of the whole album before the final run.
* Difficulty (enemy complexity) rises steadily; the *player's power* rises faster between M09–M14 (Silencer, Lance, Ripsaw, mech), creating the "I'm a god" mid-game, then Acts IV–V push back with Gilded enemies, Maestros and Carillons.

## 6. Mission Document Template

Each file in `14_Missions/` uses this structure:

1. **Header** — ID, Act, location, kit, target duration, story beats covered, new elements.
2. **Overview** — narrative purpose and player experience goal.
3. **Level Flow** — area-by-area route with IDs (`A_Mxx_nn`), dimensions, traversal, with a node graph.
4. **Encounters** — every arena in the §5.5 format of `11_AI_and_Encounter_Design.md`.
5. **Set Pieces & Scripted Events.**
6. **Secrets & Collectibles** — each with location, hint, reward.
7. **Mission Challenges** (3).
8. **Audio** — music cues and ambience.
9. **Art & Lighting** — palette, key props, landmark.
10. **Asset List** — new assets required by this mission (IDs to add to `22_Asset_Manifest.md`).

## 7. Weapon Mod Acquisition (Mod Crates)

Weapon mods are found in **Mod Crates** (`PKP_ModCrate_<Weapon>_<Mod>`): heavy Static supply crates with Wren's hand-painted wrench-and-lightning decal and an orange strobe. They sit **on or directly beside the critical path** (never in secrets) and play a short unpack animation (the Unsung rips the lid off; Wren on comms: "Ooh, that one's mine! I mean — yours.
