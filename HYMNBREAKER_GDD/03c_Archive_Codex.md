# 03c — The Archive (Codex)

## 1. Rules

* The **Archive** is the in-game codex, browsable in the pause menu and at the Quietus's Archive station. ~190 entries.
* **Unlock sources:** story progress (automatic), **Archive Stones** (violet data slates in human areas; glyph-stones in Oreth/Choir areas), first encounters with enemies/weapons/hazards (automatic), mission completion, post-credits.
* **Voices:** every entry has a **Source** line — the in-world author — which sets its tone:

| Source | Voice | Visual frame |
|---|---|---|
| **Concord Record** | Dry, bureaucratic, redacted | Stamped grey paper |
| **Lund's Notes** | Scholarly, tender, guilty, digressive | Handwritten on graph paper |
| **Static Field Guide** | Wren's voice: blunt, funny, profane, practical ("How to fight") | Photocopied zine with doodles |
| **Radio Free Static** | Harlan's on-air transcripts | Tape-label card |
| **The Canticles** | Consonance teachings: serene, persuasive | White vellum, gold rule lines |
| **Choir Record** | Translated Choir glyph-stones: impersonal, plural, beautiful | Gold staff-lines |
| **Oreth Glyph-Stone** | Translated Oreth song-script: ancient, grieving, lyrical | Pale stone rubbing |

* **Enemy entries** always end with a **"How to fight"** box (Static Field Guide voice) — accessibility requirement (`23` §3.4).
* Length: 60–220 words per entry.

## 2. Key Entries (full text)

### ARC_Obj_SilentVault — *The Silent Vault*
**Source:** Concord Record, Tranquility Deep, file TD-0001 (partially redacted)
> Object recovered 22 August 2121, 412 m below Mare Tranquillitatis. Dimensions 4.0 × 2.5 × 2.0 m. Material: unknown. Absorbs 100.0% of incident acoustic energy across all measured frequencies and 99.97% of electromagnetic radiation. Surface temperature constant at 2.7 K regardless of environment. No seams. No mass reading consistent with its volume. Personnel report headaches, tinnitus and "a feeling of being listened to." █████ personnel have requested transfer. Designation: **SILENT VAULT**. Classification: ██████. Directive: study, do not disturb. *Addendum 2194: see Lund, K. — incident report TD-2194-03 (SEALED).*

### ARC_Log_Lund_2194 — *The Ringing*
**Source:** Lund's Notes, personal log, 9 October 2194
> We did it. God forgive me, we did it. Point-eight seconds. The array pushed the Vault to resonance and for point-eight seconds it *rang* — the sensors went mad, three of the grad students were sick, the lights in Sector C burst — and inside the ringing there was a *rhythm*. Irregular. Thump — thump-thump — pause — thump. I've played it back four hundred times. It isn't mechanical. It isn't geological. It's a heartbeat, and it's broken, and it's the most alive thing I have ever heard.
> The Directorate wants it classified. Fine. I'll keep the tape.

### ARC_Tech_StaticCollar — *The Static Collar (v3)*
**Source:** Static Field Guide
> Neck rig + headset. Plays shaped noise into your skull and through your collarbones so the Hymn can't get its fingers into your rhythm. v3 has three noise modes (WHITE, PINK, and RIOT, which is just my band's demo tape on loop — it tests best). Battery lasts 30 hours. **Never let it die.** If the VU lights go dark, you have hours, not days. If you start humming along to anything pretty — anything — tell somebody. We will slap you. It's policy.
> *— W.C.*

### ARC_Fac_Static — *The Static*
**Source:** Radio Free Static, transcript
> "People ask me who the Static are. The Static is anybody who refused. Soldiers, sure. But also bus drivers. Nurses. That kid who plays drums on buckets outside the metro. The Static is every apartment with the stereo cranked at three in the morning out of pure spite. The Choir wants one song. We are a million bad songs at once. That's not a weakness, friends. That's the whole plan."

### ARC_Fac_Consonance — *The Consonance*
**Source:** The Canticles, First Verse
> *Before the Signal, we were alone. Each of us a single voice in a dark room, singing to no one. Our pain had no answer; our grief had no end. Then the sky sang to us, and we understood: we were never meant to sing alone. The Consonance is a choir that has not yet found all its voices. When the last voice joins, there will be no more loneliness, no more fear, no more loss. Take off your collar. Listen. You are forgiven.*

### ARC_Obj_Aureole — *The Aureole*
**Source:** Lund's Notes
> It's a ring — around the whole planet — of standing sound. That's the only way I can describe it. It isn't matter. It's a *chord*, held so long and so hard that it became visible. Eight notes. Seven are coming from the Spires the Choir grew across the system; the eighth is — missing. A gap on the far side. Something hasn't sung yet.
> When all eight sound together, the Aureole will close, and the chord will fall on Earth like a tide. I've run the models forty times. Collars won't matter. Walls won't matter. Minutes, not days.

### ARC_Enemy_Thrall — *Thrall*
**Source:** Static Field Guide
> These were people. Remember that, and then shoot them anyway, because they'd want you to. Thralls are the Tuned who've gone all the way: porcelain skin, mouth split open like a flower, singing the same note as every other Thrall in the neighborhood. Slow until they're not — they lunge.
> **How to fight:** Anything kills them. Punch twice and they *falter* — glowing orange — then finish them for health. Rip them with the Harrow for ammo. They're not the threat. They're your **supply line**.

### ARC_Enemy_Chorister — *Chorister*
**Source:** Static Field Guide
> Picture a choirboy made of fine china, stretched eight feet tall, crawling on the ceiling. That's a Chorister. They throw balls of sound (blue — dodge) and sometimes a **gold ring** (don't dodge — PUNCH IT BACK. Seriously. Time it right and it goes home to Daddy).
> **How to fight:** Rivet them in the head. Harrow them off the walls. Listen for the long, high note — that's the gold one coming.

### ARC_Enemy_Crescendo — *Crescendo*
**Source:** Static Field Guide
> Big. Armored face. Charges. The longer it runs, the faster and louder it gets — it's literally a crescendo, I didn't name it, the Doc did, the Doc's a nerd.
> **How to fight:** It can't turn once it's going. Wait. Wait. *Dash sideways.* It hits the wall, it's stunned, its back is open — those pipes on its back are the soft bit. A Hush Charge freezes it mid-charge if you panic. Don't panic.

### ARC_Weapon_Gravedigger — *Gravedigger*
**Source:** Static Field Guide (with a photo of Sgt. D. Ruiz taped to the page)
> Double-barrel, sawn-off, hand-carved stock, bicycle inner-tube grip. This was Ruiz's. He carried it for six years and never fired it at a person. Said it was "for emergencies." He's been tuned since March. I'd say this counts.
> Two barrels, one pull, twenty pellets, one very dead problem. Break it, reload, repeat. Get close. Closer. Ruiz would've wanted you closer.

### ARC_Hist_RadioFreeStatic — *Radio Free Static*
**Source:** Radio Free Static, transcript
> "When the Fall happened, the Concord told everyone to stay calm and stay quiet. Worst advice in history. I was in the booth at KHLC, doing the overnight. My producer started humming. I turned the board all the way up and played the loudest thing in the building — a thrash record from 2140 — and she stopped. So I never turned it off. Two hundred days later, I'm on four hundred frequencies, running on diesel, spite, and six hundred records. If you can hear me, you're still you. Stay that way."

### ARC_Obj_Shieldwarden — *"Shieldwarden"*
**Source:** Lund's Notes
> Dorian called it *Shieldwarden*. I know that word — or rather, I know that *glyph*. It's carved into the Vault's outer shell, four hundred times, in a spiral. For twenty-six years I assumed it was the name of the prison. A warning label. *Here lies the Shieldwarden* — or *Beware the Shieldwarden* — or *Shieldwarden: do not open*.
> I think now it was a title. I think it was a rank. I think I've been reading a soldier's name off the inside of their coffin.

### ARC_Hist_TheSignal — *The Signal*
**Source:** Concord Record + Radio Free Static
> 14 March 2196, 02:17 local: Dish 1 of the Listening Array records a structured signal from beyond the Kuiper Belt. Principal investigator: Dr. A. Voss. Public release: 1 June 2196.
> *Harlan, on air, years later:* "My neighbor cried when they played it on the news. I turned it off. It sounded like an ad. Too pretty. Nothing real is that pretty."

### ARC_Doc_AureliaNote — *A Note in a Drawer*
**Source:** found in Aurelia Voss's office, Voss Institute
> *It stopped hurting. For the first time in twenty years, it stopped. I listened to the recording in the dark with my eyes closed and for four minutes I wasn't a widow or a mother without a daughter or a scientist or anyone. I was just a note in something enormous and kind. Then it ended and it all came back.*
> *I have to find out where it's coming from. I have to go back.*

### ARC_Tech_MuteEngine — *The Mute Engine*
**Source:** Concord Record + Static Field Guide
> A 25-meter sphere of stacked anechoic lattices around a counter-phase resonator. Generates a field of **absolute silence** 400 m across. Built to keep the Silent Vault dormant during transport. Power draw: ██████. Cost: ██████████.
> *Wren's note:* "It's the reason we're alive. It's also the scariest room on the ship. Nobody hangs out in there. You go in, you come out with your ears ringing from NOTHING. Don't touch the blue lever. There is no blue lever. Stop looking for it."

### ARC_Hist_ConversionProcess — *How the Chancel Works*
**Source:** Lund's Notes (dictated while half-tuned)
> It isn't cruel. That's what nobody will want to hear. There's no pain. They bring you in asleep, stand you in the hall, and the Organs sing you *slowly* — weeks, sometimes — so the gilding grows evenly, like frost on a window. You dream the whole time. You dream you're forgiven. I was in there for nineteen days. I'm not ashamed to say that I miss it. I'm only ashamed that part of me still wants to go back.
